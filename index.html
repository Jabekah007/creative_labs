Interactive Prompt Transformation Engine

See the difference between a frustrating, amateur prompt and an engineered Creative Labs instruction. Select an everyday scenario below:

Amateur / Vague Input Prone to hallucinations & fluff
"Reschedule my meeting with the VP tomorrow."
Typical Disappointing Output:

"Subject: Meeting Reschedule. Dear Sir/Madam, I am writing to inform you that our meeting scheduled for tomorrow must be rescheduled due to unforeseen circumstances. Please let me know what other times work for your busy calendar..."

⚠️ Too stiff, lacks proposed alternate dates, invites unnecessary back-and-forth email friction.
Creative Labs C-R-E-F Directive 100% Deterministic & Ready
"[Role]: Executive Assistant. [Task]: Reschedule tomorrow's 2 PM sync with VP Sarah due to a flight delay. Propose Thursday at 10 AM or Friday at 2:30 PM. [Format]: 3 concise sentences, confident and professional."
Refined Production Deliverable:
"Hi Sarah — Due to an unforeseen flight delay returning from Chicago, I need to push our 2 PM check-in tomorrow. Would Thursday at 10:00 AM or Friday at 2:30 PM work for you to review the quarterly deck? Let me know which slot you prefer and I'll send an updated calendar hold."
✓ Solves scheduling in 1 interaction. Clear, respectful, and ready to send.
